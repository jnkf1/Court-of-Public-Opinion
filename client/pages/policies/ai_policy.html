AI Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. How AI Is Used

Court of Public Opinion uses an AI judge to evaluate submitted arguments. Each case is scored across four categories — logic, rebuttal, evidence, and persuasion — and the AI determines the final verdict of WON, LOST, or DRAW based on those scores.

2. Limitations

AI judging is not infallible. Scores reflect the AI's interpretation of your argument at the time it was submitted and may not always align with how a human judge would rule. The AI does not have access to information outside of what is submitted in the case.

3. Human Oversight

The scoring criteria and case topics are designed and reviewed by the site administrators. We continue to refine how the AI judge evaluates arguments to keep verdicts fair and consistent.

4. Data Used for Scoring

Only the arguments you submit within a case are used to generate your scores. Your submissions are not used to train third-party AI models.

5. Fairness

We aim for the AI judge to evaluate every debater by the same standard, regardless of stance, topic, or writing style. If you believe a verdict was scored unfairly, you're welcome to reach out to the site administrators.

6. Changes to This Policy

As the AI judging system evolves, this policy will be updated to reflect how it works. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
