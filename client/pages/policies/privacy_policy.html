Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account with Court of Public Opinion, we collect your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. When you take part in a debate, we store the case topic, the stance you argued, your submitted arguments, and the scores and verdict assigned to that case.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your account information is used to identify you, keep you signed in, and build your public debating record — your win/loss history and argument profile. We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Session Storage

After you sign up or log in, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage. This token is used to identify you on future requests without asking for your password again. It is cleared when you log out.

4. Data Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. Session tokens are randomly generated and unique to each login session.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account at any time from the My Record page. Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials and your own case history. Where deleting your account would affect someone else's record — for example, an opponent's case from a room debate against you — that record is kept, with the reference to your account removed rather than the record itself being destroyed.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the platform evolves. Continued use of Court of Public Opinion after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site administrators.
